About the villa

Privacy, garden and a pool of your own — minutes from the coast.

The house

Villa Caetano is a private 3-bedroom villa in Siolim with a spacious pool, pool house, open living areas and a large tropical garden. Quiet nights, short drives to Anjuna and Vagator. Managed by Staymaster.

The location

6–10 minutes from the coastline, about 15 minutes from Mopa Airport. Close enough for the beach, far enough for space.
