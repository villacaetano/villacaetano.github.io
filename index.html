Villa Caetano offers a rare blend of luxury, privacy, and location in the heart of North Goa. Set within a lush tropical landscape, the villa features a spacious private pool, expansive open areas, and a beautifully maintained garden that creates a serene, resort-like atmosphere. The large backyard and surrounding greenery give you a sense of space and calm that’s hard to find so close to the action.

Despite its peaceful setting, Villa Caetano is just minutes away from some of Goa’s most iconic beaches, including Anjuna and Vagator, making it perfect for both relaxation and exploration. Conveniently located only 6–10 minutes from the coastline and around 15 minutes from Mopa Airport, the villa ensures easy access without compromising on privacy.

Whether you're looking to unwind in nature or enjoy Goa’s vibrant beach life, Villa Caetano delivers the best of both worlds in one exclusive stay.
