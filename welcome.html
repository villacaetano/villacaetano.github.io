Local Recommendations

Siolim is a quiet, leafy North Goa village — peaceful, yet only a short drive from Anjuna and Vagator beaches.

Nearby favourites:

  • Hosa — highly rated modern restaurant (about 6–8 min drive)
  • One8 Commune — Virat Kohli’s riverside restaurant (about 10–15 min drive)
  • Saiba — authentic Goan food, great for lunch (about 5–7 min)
  • alag. — specialty coffee & desserts (about 5–7 min)
  • Thalassa / Kiki by the Sea — sunset & riverside dining (about 10–15 min)

What is the best way to reach the villa?

The villa is 22.6 km from Mopa Airport, 15.7 km from Thivim Railway Station and 8.5 km from Mapusa Bus Stand. We can help you with more information on how to get here once you have booked.

Can I drive all the way up to the entrance?

Yes, you can drive right up to the entrance of the villa.

Is parking available?

Yes, parking is available for up to 5–6 cars.
